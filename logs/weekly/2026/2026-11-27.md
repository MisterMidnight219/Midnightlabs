## Completed wins

### 1. Activated and validated an Ellie native-firewall pilot

**Status:** Operational evidence; controlled pilot, not a completed production security boundary.

**Objective:** Replace a permissive virtualization-host baseline with a native, reviewable firewall policy while preserving running workloads and a recoverable management path.

**Implemented and tested:**

- Captured and hashed the existing host and guest-firewall baseline before activation.
- Enabled the native firewall for IPv4 and IPv6 and confirmed the service reached `enabled/running`.
- Validated the committed policy hash and disarmed the independent rollback only after the verified state was observed.
- Left existing guest policies unchanged during the host activation.
- Exercised the sandbox start interlock four ways: missing or empty quarantine state rejected the real start path, while the exact restored policy was accepted.
- Verified that production workloads remained running during the interlock tests.
- Built an isolated Proxmox-like network test and confirmed expected reachability, identity resolution, and cleanup without deploying Forge.

**Failure and recovery lesson supported by the record:** A service reporting “running” did not mean a policy was active. The original state was effectively permissive despite installed firewall components and selected NIC flags. Activation therefore required baseline capture, candidate validation, integrity checks, an external rollback path, and explicit observation of the post-change state.
