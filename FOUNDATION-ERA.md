# MidnightLabs Foundation Era

The **Foundation Era** covers the first documented MidnightLabs build cycle in May and June 2025. These artifacts capture the transition from conceptual networking knowledge into hands-on simulation, basic enterprise services, Cisco IOS navigation, and structured technical reflection.

They are preserved because the learning process matters—not because every early artifact represents the present state of the lab.

## What came forward

The Foundation Era established patterns that later became MidnightLabs doctrine:

- Learn by interacting with a real or simulated system.
- Record failures instead of hiding them.
- Use metaphors to build durable mental models.
- Treat topology and relationships as seriously as individual devices.
- Turn lab work into evidence that another person can inspect.
- Distinguish what exists from what is merely planned.

The next major documentation era centers on the physical home-lab ecosystem: Midgar, Ellie, Index Prime, observability, and eventually AIDA.
